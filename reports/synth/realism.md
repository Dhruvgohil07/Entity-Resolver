# Synthetic catalog: how hard its duplicates are

A catalog generated by `synth/` is only evidence if its duplicates are as hard as
real ones. Every statistic below is computed identically — by `synth/realism.py`,
through `normalize` — on the seeds and on the generated catalog.

Regenerate with:

```bash
python -m dedup.synth.generate --seed-dataset abt-buy --records 20000 --seed 0 --out data/synth/abt-buy-train-20k --report reports/synth/realism.md
```

## Setup

- Seeds: the train side of `abt-buy`'s entity-grouped split — 1,521 records in 755 entities, 777 true pairs.
- Catalog: 18,829 records in 8,401 entities derived from 755 seed families, 21,284 true pairs. 618 requested siblings found no free code or title of either kind and were dropped, so the catalog holds 94.1% of its 20,000-record target.
- `records.jsonl` SHA-256: `cba0c2fb1b13a4b87bd045901c284ddde2c2be1540371e93034dfcdc23ea9ed7`.

## Reading this honestly

- **Every calibrated statistic is within ±0.05 of the seeds.** The widest gap is price gap, median
  at -0.013. The corruption rates were tuned by hand until this held, against the seeds themselves
  — the seed dataset's train split — so it shows the generator can reproduce these statistics, not
  that its duplicates are right in every way that matters.
- **The seeds are a train split, so their test split never reached this catalog.**
  `test_fraction=0.3`, `seed=0` is the split every report on `abt-buy` is scored on, so a model
  trained here can still be scored on that test split without having seen it.
- **Brand is on both listings of 26.3% of synthetic pairs against 1.0% of the seeds', by design.**
  The generator withholds a brand per listing, independently. A seed catalog assembled from
  sources with different columns makes brand co-occurrence a property of which sources a pair
  spans, and that structure is not reproduced — so a brand column carries more signal here than on
  the seeds.
- **Listings of one product differ less in length than the seeds' do** (token-count ratio 0.776
  against 0.723). Corruption drops and borrows words per listing; it does not reproduce one source
  writing systematically longer titles than another.
- **Sibling density matches the seeds** (0.512 against the seeds' 0.557), counted as a same-brand
  code within two edits. That holds for the rate, not the shape: every synthetic sibling is
  derived from one seed, where the seeds' siblings are independent products.
- **Entities are larger than any the seeds have.** The seeds' largest entity holds 3 records; here
  1,269 entities hold more, up to 8. That is deliberate — whether pricing unemitted pairs at p = 0
  under-merges large entities cannot be asked of pairs and triples — and the size distribution is
  a judgment call, not a measurement.
